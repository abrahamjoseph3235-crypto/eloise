Before you choose

  • Confirm the professional's identity, licence or registration, and scope of practice.
  • Ask about experience with your concerns, confidentiality, session format and cancellation policy.
  • Confirm the full fee and session length before booking.
  • This directory is not an emergency service or a substitute for urgent help.
