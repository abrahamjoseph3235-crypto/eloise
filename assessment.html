Extra care for young people

Eloise plans to support adults and minors, but the minor-specific assessment and professional-review service are not enabled in this prototype. Please do not enter assessment answers here. Before launch, Eloise must establish age-appropriate consent, confidentiality limits, qualified child-and-adolescent professional review, safeguarding escalation, and a safe way to involve a parent or guardian when required by law and the young person's circumstances.

If you feel unsafe or need help now, speak to a trusted adult or qualified local professional. If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department. Eloise is not an emergency service.
