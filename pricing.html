Therapy session fees

Each provider sets their own fee and must display it before a client books. The platform's proposed commission is 10% of the eligible completed session fee.

Example: ₦10,000 completed session
Provider gross share (90%)₦9,000
Eloise commission (10%)₦1,000

Illustration only. Separately disclosed payment-processing costs, taxes, refunds or other agreed adjustments may change the final payout.

Important safeguards

  • Never charge for essential screening feedback or urgent support information
  • Never present automated output as a diagnosis
  • Professional reviewers must be credential-checked and work within their scope
  • For minors, age-appropriate consent and safeguarding procedures must be ready before assessment services launch
  • Assessment information is not shared with a therapist without separate, informed consent

Not available for purchase yet

The check-in, professional interpretation and booking/payment flows are prototypes. Do not submit real health information or payment details. Live services require a secure backend, verified professionals, legal review, consent flows and operational safeguarding procedures.
