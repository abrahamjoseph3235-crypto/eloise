Frequently asked questions

Choose a topic to read more.

For clients

How does provider matching work?

You can share preferences such as language, time zone and session format. A qualified team member should review matching needs before care begins.

Are assessments a diagnosis?

No. Screening questionnaires can help structure a conversation, but they do not replace an assessment or diagnosis by a qualified professional.

Can I change an appointment?

The live rescheduling and cancellation rules will be shown before confirmation. The current website is a front-end prototype and does not yet change bookings.

How do I view my results?

The client space previews assessment history and results. Live records require a secure account and a connected backend.

For providers

Which documents are needed?

Requirements depend on the country and professional regulator. Eloise must publish a verified checklist for each supported jurisdiction.

How does the interview process work?

The intended workflow includes application review, credential verification, an interview and an onboarding decision.

Where can I view my schedule?

The provider workspace preview includes a schedule and availability interface. It is not connected to a live booking system yet.

How are payments handled?

Payment and payout terms must be confirmed in the published provider agreement. No payment provider is connected to this prototype.
